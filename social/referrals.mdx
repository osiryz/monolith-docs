Monofin's referral program is simple and permanent: share your code, both sides win, forever.

## How it works

| | What they get |
|---|---|
| **Your friend** (joins with your code) | **10% discount** on eligible Monofin fees |
| **You** (the referrer) | **25% of the trading fees** they generate — for life |

- Your code can be applied at sign-up — one tap from the referral center.
- Rewards accrue automatically as your referrals trade, across all market types.
- There's no cap on how many people you can refer.

## The referral center

One tap from home (the gift icon), the referral center shows:

- **Your code and link** — ready to share anywhere
- **Live earnings** — what your referrals have generated, updated as they trade
- **Your referral tree** — who's joined, who's active

## Built into the product

Referrals aren't a separate campaign — they're woven into how Monofin spreads:

- **PnL share cards** carry your code automatically — every victory lap recruits
- **Trade links** attribute sign-ups to you
- Your profile is public proof — the best referral pitch is your track record

<Note>
Referral rewards are paid from Monofin's builder-fee revenue, not skimmed from your friends — their 10% discount and your 25% share both come out of Monofin's side of the fee.
</Note>
