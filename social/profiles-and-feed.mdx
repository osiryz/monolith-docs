**FOMO, but with receipts.**

Monofin is a trading app with a pulse. Your handle is your identity, your profile is your track record, and your feed is the market's group chat — with receipts.

## Usernames and profiles

At onboarding you pick a **username** — your handle across the app: leaderboard, feed, chat, trade links. Connect your **X handle** to bring your existing identity and credibility with you.

Your public profile shows your positions, PnL, and trading history. Add an avatar and you're a person, not a wallet address.

## The feed

Follow traders. Your feed fills with their moves:

- **Entries and exits** as they happen — market, price, timestamp
- **Theses** — when a trader attaches their reasoning, you see the *why*, not just the *what*
- **Filters by asset** to cut the noise

The feed is where conviction goes public. Someone you respect just bought NO at 70¢ on the market everyone's bullish on? That's signal a chart can't give you.

## Share your PnL

Generate **shareable trade cards** for any position or result — the chart, the fills, the PnL, rendered for social. Your referral code rides on every card, so victory laps recruit for you. See [Referrals](/social/referrals).

## Trade links

Share a **prefilled trade setup** with anyone: the market, the side, the sizing logic. They open it, review it in their own account, confirm or pass. Ideas travel. Execution stays personal.

## Privacy controls

Positions are public by default — it's what makes the leaderboard and feed meaningful. Rather trade quietly? Adjust visibility in **Settings → Profile**. Your balances are never public, whatever you choose.
