Trading on Monofin is a spectator sport. The leaderboard ranks the best traders in public — with real positions and real PnL, not hot takes.

## The boards

- **Top traders by PnL** — the headline board, ranked by profit over the window
- **Weekly and monthly windows** — long-term legends and this week's hot hand are different people; both boards exist
- **Per-market-type boards** — perps traders and event-market forecasters compete on their own turf

## Trader profiles

Tap any name on the board to open their public profile:

- **Account value and all-time PnL**
- **Volume traded**
- **Open positions** — what they're holding right now
- **Follow button** — add them to your feed

Every position on a profile is real and timestamped. When someone talks their book on Monofin, you can check the book.

## Why trade in public?

Because reputation compounds. Consistent performers collect followers, and followers copy trades, share your cards, and join your referral tree. On Monofin, being right in public is an asset.

<Note>
Your public visibility is a choice. Positions are public by default so the leaderboard means something — you can adjust visibility in **Settings → Profile**.
</Note>
