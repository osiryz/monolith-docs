## The home board

Four tabs:

| Tab | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Events** | Live event markets — the outcome, the price in cents, the 24-hour move, when it resolves |
| **Perps** | Top movers by volume and % change — BTC, ETH, HYPE, and more |
| **RWA** | Real-world asset markets — stocks, commodities, FX |
| **Watchlist** | Every market you've starred, across all three types |

Every card obeys one rule: **one number, one move, one reason.** Scan the board in seconds. Know what moved and why.

## One balance, every market

All three market types trade from the same USDC. Win on an election market Tuesday, rotate into a BTC perp Wednesday, park what's idle in [Earn](/earn/what-is-monofin-earn) Thursday. No transfers. No sub-accounts.

## How markets get listed

On Monofin, markets are **config, not code**. New markets are reviewed and enabled from a curated registry — every listing ships with written resolution rules, a category, and jurisdiction settings before it appears. When a venue launches a market, Monofin can list it the same day.

<Note>
Not every market is available everywhere. Some categories — sports event markets, for one — are restricted in certain jurisdictions, and the board only shows what's available where you live. See [Availability](/account/availability).
</Note>
