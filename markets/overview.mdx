## The home board

Your home board has four tabs:

| Tab | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Events** | Live event markets — the outcome, the price in cents, the 24-hour move, and when it resolves |
| **Perps** | Top movers by volume and % change — BTC, ETH, HYPE, and more |
| **RWA** | Real-world asset markets — stocks, commodities, FX |
| **Watchlist** | Every market you've starred, across all three types |

Every market card follows the same rule: **one number, one move, one reason.** You should be able to scan the board in seconds and know exactly what changed and why.

## One balance, every market

All three market types trade from the same USDC balance. Win on an election market on Tuesday, rotate into a BTC perp on Wednesday, park what's idle in [Earn](/earn/what-is-monofin-earn) on Thursday — no transfers, no sub-accounts.

## How markets get listed

Markets on Monofin are **config, not code**. New markets are reviewed and enabled by the Monofin team from a curated registry — every listing has defined resolution rules, a category, and jurisdiction settings before it appears in the app. When a venue launches a new market, Monofin can list it the same day.

<Note>
Not every market is available everywhere. Some categories — sports event markets, for example — are restricted in certain jurisdictions, and the board only shows you what's available where you live. See [Availability](/account/availability).
</Note>
