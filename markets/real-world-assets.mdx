The RWA tab brings traditional markets on-chain: stocks, indices, commodities, currencies. Around the clock — not during exchange hours.

## What you can trade

- **Stocks** — major US and international names
- **Commodities** — gold, silver, oil, and more
- **Indices** — broad market exposure
- **FX** — major currency pairs

RWA markets on Monofin are perpetual-style instruments from specialist on-chain deployers (Hyperliquid's HIP-3 ecosystem). They track the underlying market's price — exposure without a brokerage account, a share certificate, or a market-hours window.

## Why it matters

- **Trade NVIDIA on a Sunday night.** Traditional exchanges close. These markets don't.
- **One balance.** Your gold position and your election-market position settle to the same USDC.
- **Same tools as perps.** Direction, size, leverage, TP/SL, market and limit orders — the ticket you already know.

## Things to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Prices track underlying market hours">
    RWA markets trade 24/7, but the underlying reference markets still open and close. Liquidity and price confidence peak while the traditional market is open; expect wider spreads off-hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="These are derivatives, not shares">
    You get price exposure, not ownership — no dividends, no voting rights, no share transfer. The instrument tracks the underlying price and settles in USDC.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Availability varies by jurisdiction">
    Some real-world asset markets are restricted in certain regions. Your board only shows what's available where you live. See [Availability](/account/availability).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
