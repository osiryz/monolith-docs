Monofin is **non-custodial**. Not as a policy — as architecture. Your funds live in a wallet only you control. Monofin never holds your assets.

## Your embedded wallet

Sign up with **email, Apple, or Google** and an embedded wallet is created for you — powered by Privy, the wallet infrastructure trusted across the industry. No seed phrase to write down. No browser extension to install.

- **Only you can sign.** Every trade, deposit, and withdrawal is signed from your wallet. Monofin can't move your funds — the keys aren't ours to use.
- **Your balance is yours.** Withdraw everything, anytime, to any address you control — even if you never open the app again.

## Securing your account

- **Biometric app lock** — Face ID on iOS, biometrics on Android. A borrowed phone isn't a borrowed wallet.
- **Sign-in security** — your account ties to your email or Apple/Google identity; enable two-factor there for an extra layer
- **Push alerts** — fills, liquidations, account activity. Nothing happens silently.

## The rules nobody can bend

<Warning>
- **Monofin will never ask for your password, recovery credentials, or a "verification deposit."** Not in chat, not by email, not by DM. Anyone who does is a scammer — report and block.
- **Chat is for talk, not advice.** Moderators are badged; impersonators are not. See [Chat](/social/chat).
- **Double-check withdrawal addresses.** Crypto transfers are final. Verify the address and network before confirming.
</Warning>

## If something looks wrong

Email **support@monofin.xyz** from the address tied to your account. For chat safety issues, use in-app report — it goes to a human moderation queue with a 24-hour review SLA.
