title: "Availability"
description: "Where Monofin is available, and why the markets you see depend on where you live."
---

Monofin is available in most of the world — but not everywhere, and not every market everywhere. What you can trade depends on where you live, and the app is built to show you exactly that.

## How it works

- **At sign-up**, you confirm your jurisdiction. From then on, every surface in the app — the home board, market pages, Earn — only shows what's available to you.
- **Some categories are restricted in some regions.** Sports event markets, for example, are unavailable in certain jurisdictions. If a market isn't on your board, that's why.
- **You never have to guess.** Markets you can't trade don't appear; there's no "error at checkout" moment.

## United States

Monofin's initial launch does not serve the United States — the perpetuals liquidity layer geoblocks US users. A US product built on regulated prediction-market infrastructure is on the roadmap. If you're in the US, join the waitlist and we'll tell you the moment your market opens.

## Using Monofin while traveling

Your market availability follows your actual jurisdiction, not your account's. If you travel to a restricted region, some markets may become unavailable until you return.

<Warning>
Using a VPN or any other method to disguise your jurisdiction violates Monofin's terms and can result in account restrictions. The gating exists to keep the platform — and your account — on the right side of the rules that protect it.
</Warning>

## Questions about your region?

Write to **support@monofin.xyz** with your country and the market you're trying to access, and we'll tell you its status.
