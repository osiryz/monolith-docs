One USDC balance powers everything on Monofin: event markets, perps, real-world assets, Earn. Deposit with crypto or cash. Withdraw to your own address anytime.

## Deposits

### Crypto

Send **USDC** from any supported chain to your Monofin deposit address. Most crypto deposits land in minutes.

<Tip>
The deposit address belongs to the embedded wallet only you control. Double-check the network before you send.
</Tip>

### Cards and bank transfer

No crypto yet? Buy USDC right in the app through the on-ramp:

- **Debit and credit cards** — fastest; funds typically land in minutes.
- **Bank transfer** — higher limits, typically 1–3 business days depending on your bank and region.

Card and bank purchases may require a one-time identity check with the on-ramp provider. Availability varies by region.

## Withdrawals

Withdraw USDC to **any address you control**, anytime. No lock-ups on your cash balance. No minimums beyond network fees.

<Warning>
Funds sitting in open positions or Earn vaults need to come out first — close the position or withdraw from the vault. Vault withdrawals follow each vault's terms and available liquidity. See [Monofin Earn](/earn/what-is-monofin-earn).
</Warning>

## Where your money lives

Monofin is **non-custodial**:

- Your balance sits in an embedded wallet only you control. Monofin never holds your assets.
- Every trade, deposit, and withdrawal is signed from your wallet.
- You can move everything out anytime — even if you never open the app again.

See [Wallet and security](/account/wallet-and-security) for how the wallet works and how to lock down your account.

## Fees

Monofin charges nothing on deposits or withdrawals. Network gas fees and on-ramp provider fees are shown before you confirm. Trading fees are itemized on every ticket — see [Fees](/trading/fees).
