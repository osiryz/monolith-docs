## Any market, any time

Three kinds of markets live on one home board, with new listings every week. See [Markets](/markets/overview):

- **Event markets** — politics, sports, crypto milestones, macro prints, culture. Each outcome trades between $0 and $1: a probability you can buy.
- **Crypto perpetuals** — BTC, ETH, HYPE, and more. Long or short with leverage. No expiry, no lock-ups.
- **Real-world assets** — stocks, indices, commodities, currencies. Around the clock, not just when Wall Street is open.

Event markets work like this: buy the outcome you think happens. Right? Each share pays $1. The market says 35¢ and you say it happens? That's a trade. You can also sell before resolution to lock a gain or cut a loss.

Perpetuals track the underlying asset's price, so you get long or short exposure without owning the coin, share, or metal. Open or close in seconds — nights, weekends, and holidays included, when traditional exchanges are dark.

## How trading works

Every trade on Monofin is three choices:

1. **Side.** Think it happens? Buy YES. Think it doesn't? Buy NO. On perps: long or short.
2. **Size.** How much you put behind the view. Minimums are small — test the thesis before you size it.
3. **Multiplier** (perps only). Leverage up to the market's maximum — 40x on majors. $100 at 10x controls $1,000. Multipliers magnify losses exactly like gains.

Then Monofin hands you the tools to run the trade:

- **Market and limit orders**, with liquidation price and every fee itemized before you confirm.
- **Take profit and stop loss** attached at entry, so the exit is automated from the start.
- **Close anytime.** No lock-ups, no settlement windows. One tap, 24/7.
- **Plain-English risk** on every ticket. You never guess what you're risking — and the full picture lives in [Risk disclosure](/account/risk).

Perps use funding payments between longs and shorts to stay anchored to the underlying market, and a position gets liquidated if its margin runs out. [Trading](/trading/how-trading-works) covers leverage, funding, and liquidations in detail.

## One account, every venue

Under the hood, Monofin routes each market to deep on-chain liquidity — event markets on venues like Polymarket and Hyperliquid, perps on Hyperliquid, real-world assets through specialist on-chain deployers. You never think about any of it. One login. One USDC balance. One set of markets. One price feed. Monofin moves funds between venues for you.

Monofin is **non-custodial**. Your funds live in a wallet only you control. Every trade and transfer is signed from that wallet. Monofin never holds your assets. Sign in with email, Apple, or Google — no seed phrase, no extension.

## Monofin vs going direct

Honest trade-offs. Monofin is an aggregation and social layer on top of venues you could use directly — here's what that means:

| | Monofin | Going direct to a venue |
|---|---|---|
| **Markets** | Event markets + perps + RWAs, one balance | One venue's markets only |
| **Social** | Leaderboard, public profiles, chat, follows | None |
| **Fees** | Venue fee + small builder fee ([itemized](/trading/fees)) | Venue fee only |
| **Custody** | Non-custodial | Non-custodial |
| **App** | Mobile-native, web, PWA — one login for everything | Varies by venue |
| **Best for** | Traders who want every market and the social layer | Single-venue specialists optimizing the last basis point |

If you only ever trade one venue's markets and fees matter more than features, direct might suit you better. If you want every market that matters, one balance, and to see who's actually been right — that's the thing we're building.
