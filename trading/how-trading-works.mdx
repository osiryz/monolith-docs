Election market or BTC perp — every trade on Monofin is three choices. The ticket looks different for event markets and perps. The logic is the same.

## The three choices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Side">
    Event markets: **YES** or **NO** on the outcome. Perps: **long** (price rises) or **short** (price falls).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Size">
    How much USDC backs the view. Minimums are small — test the thesis before you size it. On event markets, your size buys shares at the current price: your max loss is what you paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Multiplier (perps only)">
    Leverage, up to the market's maximum. Your collateral controls a position worth multiplier × collateral. Higher multiplier, closer liquidation price. See [Leverage, funding, and liquidations](/trading/leverage-funding-liquidations).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you confirm

The ticket shows everything before you commit:

- **Entry price** — and for limit orders, the price you're willing to accept
- **Estimated liquidation price** (perps) — where your margin runs out
- **Itemized fees** — every fee on the trade, in dollars, not fine print
- **A plain-English risk line** — what you can lose, stated like a human

Balance short? The confirm button tells you the exact shortfall — "deposit $10.72 more" — and takes you straight to funding. No dead-end errors.

## After you're in

- **Take profit / stop loss.** Attach the exit when you enter. TP/SL covers the whole position and cancels automatically when it closes.
- **Add or reduce.** Scale up or down without closing.
- **Close anytime.** No lock-ups, no settlement windows — 24/7, weekends and holidays included. Close one position or all of them from Portfolio.

## Where to see it all

**Portfolio** is your command center: cash balance, open positions across event markets, perps, and RWA, open orders, Earn positions, full transaction history.
