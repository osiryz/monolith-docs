Your trading balance shouldn't sit still between trades. **Monofin Earn** puts idle USDC to work in curated on-chain yield vaults — without leaving the app, without giving up custody.

## How it works

1. **Pick a vault.** Earn curates vaults from established DeFi protocols and professional curators (Aave, Morpho, and others), organized into three risk tiers.
2. **Deposit from your balance.** Funds flow from your own wallet to the vault contract — non-custodial throughout. You direct every deposit explicitly.
3. **Earn variable yield.** Yield comes from real economic activity — primarily borrowers paying interest to lenders. Rates move with the market.
4. **Withdraw anytime.** No fixed lock-ups, subject to each vault's terms and available liquidity. Withdrawals land back in your unified balance, ready to trade.

## Every vault card tells you

- **Where the yield comes from**, in one sentence — "borrowers pay interest to lenders"
- **The curator** and their track record
- **Withdrawal terms** — "no fixed lock-ups, subject to available liquidity"
- **Fees** — the vault's own fee structure, disclosed up front
- **A risk badge** — Tier 1, 2, or 3. See [Risk tiers](/earn/risk-tiers)

## The rules, in plain sight

<Warning>
- **Yield is variable and indicative — never guaranteed.** Rates move with the market.
- **These are DeFi protocols.** Smart-contract risk, curator risk, and market risk are real. Deposits are not insured. Losses are possible.
- **Monofin curates and surfaces vaults; it does not manage them.** Yield is generated by independent third-party protocols.
- **Never "staking," never "investment."** Earn is DeFi yield, and it's labeled that way on purpose.
</Warning>

## Who it's for

Earn is for the USDC you're not trading right now — the balance between ideas. Actively trading? Keep your margin liquid. Parked? Parking shouldn't pay 0%.

Read [Risk tiers](/earn/risk-tiers) before you deposit.
