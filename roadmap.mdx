Monofin ships in public. This page is the honest version of where the product is going: what's live today, the version roadmap ahead, and what we're exploring. No dates — priorities follow what traders actually use, and everything here can move. What just shipped: the [Changelog](/changelog).

<Note>
Everything under "Version roadmap" and "Exploring" is forward-looking. Version numbers are indicative, not committed — scope, timing, and sequencing change. Nothing on this page is a commitment, and none of it is financial advice.
</Note>

## Live now

The foundation, shipped from v1.0 through v1.9: every market that matters, one balance, the best traders in public.

- **Event markets, perps, and real-world assets** on one home board
- **Perps trading terminal** — live prices, order book, candles, pro ticket, positions, and customizable desk layouts
- **Non-custodial embedded wallet** — one USDC balance across everything
- **Full market discovery** — native markets, builder-deployed perps (HIP-3), and outcome markets (HIP-4), with categories and watchlists
- **Leaderboard with calibration badges and Rising Stars** — real positions, real PnL, real receipts
- **Signal** — the live feed with theses and a research rail (web-first)
- **Trader profiles and copy positions** — detailed on-chain track records; mirror a position with review-and-confirm
- **Monofin Earn** — curated, non-custodial yield vaults with live yield tracking (yields are variable, never guaranteed)
- **Character program** — your avatar grows with lifetime points; spend points in the gear shop on real perks like fee discounts
- **Real numbers only** — portfolio PnL from the real ledger, history and CSV export, no demo balances
- **Market discussion threads** — sharp talk under every market, next to the ticket
- **Referrals** — 10% off for friends, 25% lifetime fee share for you

## Version roadmap

What's next, version by version. Numbers and scope can move — this is the current plan of record.

### v1.10 — Your desk, your layout

- **Detachable workspaces** — pop the terminal, prediction, and portfolio panels out into their own windows
- **Categorized navigation** — Predict by category, Perps by symbol
- **Earn hardening** — stronger deposit verification and verified withdrawals

### v1.11 — Mobile, for real

- **iOS and Android general release** — out of beta, into the stores
- **Signal and chat on mobile** — the feed and trollbox, native
- Push alerts on fills, liquidations, and the markets you watch; biometric lock

### v1.12 — Outcome trading on HyperCore

- **HIP-4 outcome markets become tradable** — discovery shipped in v1.9; trading is next
- Fully collateralized, $0–$1 contracts settling on the same stack as perps — one margin system, one balance
- Event-market exposure without leaving the Monofin wallet

### v1.13 — Auto-copy

From manual copy positions to real follow-through:

- **Follow a trader with automatic mirroring** — their entries and exits, proportionally sized to your allocation
- **Your rules** — allocation caps, category filters, max-drawdown stops, instant or delayed mirroring
- Built on public track records: calibration and consistency, not one hot week

### v1.14 — One balance, truly

- **Deposit USDC from any supported chain** — QR and multi-chain deposits already live; this adds the routing brain behind them
- **Auto-routing at trade time** — funds move to the right venue when you trade, in one confirmation. You never think about chains
- Same non-custodial architecture throughout — your wallet signs, always

### v1.15 — Earn, deeper

- **Tier 3 unlocks** — behind the risk quiz, as it should be
- More curators and vaults across the tiers
- Richer yield reporting and vault terms in-line

### v2.0 — The US product

- **Regulated event markets for US users** — built on regulated prediction-market infrastructure, off the waitlist
- The biggest market we can't serve yet, and the one with the most defined path. See [Availability](/account/availability)

## Exploring

The pipeline beyond the roadmap. These are the directions we're seriously investigating — some will ship, some will change shape, some will die in review.

### Monofin AI — your research desk

An AI analyst built into the app, trained on the markets Monofin lists:

- **Research on demand** — market recaps, news catalysts, and context for any market on the board
- **Prediction models** — AI-generated probability estimates you can hold up against the market's price. When the model says 60% and the market says 40¢, that's a conversation worth having
- **Positioning intelligence** — whale positioning, funding, open interest — where the crowd is before you trade
- **Portfolio context** — it knows your balances and positions, and flags risk before you find it the hard way
- **Proposed trades, your call** — when an idea looks right, it proposes a specific trade with size and exits. Every one ends in Confirm or Cancel. Execution stays with you

Research, not advice. The models inform; they don't promise.

### Points, seasons, and the character program

The character program is live. What comes next:

- **Seasons** — time-boxed point races with their own rewards and standings
- **More gear, more perks** — the shop grows with the program
- Designed to reward consistent, genuine activity — not mercenary volume

What points become long-term is intentionally unsaid for now. Early activity is on the record.

### Trader funds — copy trading, sized properly

Beyond auto-copy: allocate to a trader's actual strategy as a fund.

- **Funds run by proven Monofin traders** — allocate USDC, mirror their entries and exits proportionally
- **Configurable allocation** — size, category filters, drawdown caps, instant or delayed
- **Receipts first** — every fund's track record is public leaderboard history, calibration included — not a pitch deck
- **Your money, your custody** — non-custodial allocation, withdraw per each fund's terms
- **Not Earn** — Earn vaults are DeFi yield from lending. Funds are active trading strategies with active trading risk. Different animal, labeled differently

### Turbo mode — 1000x, fees only when you win

A different fee shape for high-leverage trades, proven by products like Rollbit and Papertrade:

- **Win-only fees** — nothing to open, nothing to close, nothing on a loss. Winners share a cut of the profit. Lose? The loss is all you pay
- **Synthetic execution** — trades settle at oracle mid prices against on-chain liquidity: no order book, no spread, no slippage, no funding
- **Venue-routed, not house-booked** — we're exploring routing to existing synthetic venues rather than running our own LP. Monofin stays the frontend, never the counterparty
- **Its own lane** — separate ticket, stake-capped sizing, and its own stats. Turbo results never touch leaderboard PnL or calibration
- **Hard gates** — aggressive jurisdiction gating and a risk flow built for what 1000x actually is. This ships only if it can be done as trading, not gambling

### Smarter discovery

Calibration badges shipped in v1.0. The next step is depth:

- **Discovery filters beyond PnL** — consistency across multiple windows, win rate by category, risk-adjusted returns, drawdown history
- The goal: find the traders worth following before the crowd does — and avoid the ones one lucky week made famous

### Portfolio intelligence

One balance means your risks can interact in non-obvious ways. A unified portfolio view that shows you the whole picture:

- **Cross-market exposure** — your election YES, your BTC long, and your gold position in one risk view
- **Margin usage across positions** — total leverage, not per-position tunnel vision
- **Correlation flags** — when your "diversified" positions are secretly the same bet

### Execution transparency

Aggregation only wins if fills stay honest — so we show the receipts:

- **Fill quality stats** — average slippage versus venue mid, fill rates, routing outcomes
- **Routing logic in the open** — how orders get routed across venues, and why
- If going direct ever beats us on execution, you should be able to see it. That's the point of publishing it

### Signal board

Cross-venue analytics no single-venue app can build: divergences between prediction venues, mispriced mirrors of the same event, spread histories. When two venues disagree on the same outcome, one of them is wrong — that gap is the product.

### Learn

Two audiences land on Monofin: casual event-market traders and leveraged perp veterans. Each deserves an education layer built for the other one's world — event-market mechanics for perp traders, leverage and liquidation math for first-time forecasters. In-product, in the docs, in plain English.

### Leagues

Trading as a team sport: clans, seasons, and head-to-head competition on top of the public leaderboard. Bring your group chat, keep the receipts.

### The long list

Further out, in rough order of distance: fiat card for spending your balance, OTC desk access for large traders, a marketplace for market-adjacent goods and services. These are direction, not plan.

## How features get decided

Every feature runs the same gauntlet before it ships: does it strengthen the core loop (discover → trade → share → return), what does it cost in compliance and moderation, and can we kill it with a config flag if we're wrong. The features above made it onto this page by passing the first gate. They ship by passing the rest. Have a view on what we should build next? That's very on-brand.
