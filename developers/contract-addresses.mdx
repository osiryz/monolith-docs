Monofin is a **non-custodial frontend**. Most of what your wallet signs goes to venue contracts, not Monofin contracts. This page lists all of them, so you can verify exactly what you're approving before you sign.

## Monofin contracts

Monofin owns **no contracts that hold user funds** today. Perps settle on Hyperliquid, event markets settle on the underlying prediction-market venue, and Earn deposits go directly from your wallet to third-party vault contracts.

If Monofin ever deploys its own contracts (for example, Earn wrappers or on-chain referral accounting), they will be:

1. Audited, with the report linked here.
2. Published on this page **before** any user funds can touch them.
3. Owned by a timelock behind a multisig — no instant admin actions.

## Perpetuals and real-world assets — Hyperliquid

Perps and RWA markets run on Hyperliquid. There is **no exchange contract to call**: orders are signed actions on HyperCore, Hyperliquid's L1. Monofin's builder fee attaches through a Hyperliquid builder code, which you approve once from your wallet — you can revoke it anytime.

| Contract | Chain | Address |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit bridge (Bridge2) | Arbitrum One | `0x2Df1c51E09aECF9cacB7bc98cB1742757f163dF7` |
| USDC (HyperEVM) | HyperEVM | `0xb88339CB7199b77E23DB6E890353E22632Ba630f` |

<Warning>
The minimum deposit to the Hyperliquid bridge is **5 USDC**. Smaller transfers are not credited and cannot be recovered. Deposits from other chains route through Circle CCTP — the app handles this for you.
</Warning>

Full venue reference: [Hyperliquid docs](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs).

## Verify before you sign

- Every address on this page is also published in the venue's own documentation. Cross-check before approving anything.
- Monofin will never DM you an address. If a "new contract" appears anywhere except this page, treat it as hostile.
- Questions about an approval your wallet is asking for? Email **support@monofin.xyz** before you sign.
