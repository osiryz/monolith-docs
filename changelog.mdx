## v1.0.0 — October 23, 2026

The first production release of Monofin. The web app is live at [app.monofin.xyz](https://app.monofin.xyz).

**New**

- **Home board** — Events / Perps / RWA / Watchlist tabs with trending sections, resolution countdowns, and search
- **Market pages** — canonical market links built for sharing, each with a resolution rules panel, discussion, and a smart-money rail
- **Leaderboard 2.0** — board modes, category filters, **calibration badges**, and Rising Stars
- **Signal** — the live feed, web-first, with a research rail on market pages
- **Wallet** — balance, deposits, transaction history, and settings in one screen
- **Perps terminal** — live prices, order book, candles, a pro ticket, positions panel, and an all-pairs picker
- **Referral center** — live from day one

**Under the hood**

- Real authentication with per-user wallets, identical across web and mobile
- Card and bank on-ramp deposits
- Live market data across perps and event markets

**Notes**

- Earn tiers render in preview while vault integrations finalize — see [Monofin Earn](/earn/what-is-monofin-earn)
- Signal is web-first; mobile follows
- iOS and Android apps are in beta
