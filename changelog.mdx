What shipped, when. Newest first.

## v1.9.0 — October 26, 2026

Full market discovery and copy positions.

**New**

- **Market discovery** — browse every market source in one place: native markets, HIP-3 builder-deployed perps (including real-world assets), and HIP-4 outcome markets — with categories and watchlists
- **Trader profiles** — detailed on-chain track records and position history for any public trader
- **Copy positions** — mirror a trader's position straight from their profile, with a review-and-confirm step and a full audit record
- **Homepage layout controls** — arrange the board the way you trade

**Notes**

- HIP-4 outcome markets are discovery-only in this release
- Automatic copy mirroring is not included — every copy is an explicit, confirmed action

## v1.8.0 — October 26, 2026

**Under the hood**

- Internal operations dashboard for support and market ops — faster answers when something looks wrong

## v1.7.0 — October 26, 2026

**Improved**

- The perps terminal connects to **live accounts** — real positions, real balances
- **Synced layouts** — your terminal setup follows you across devices

## v1.6.1 — October 26, 2026

**Fixed**

- Terminal order book stays in sync; simplified fee display

## v1.6.0 — October 26, 2026

A trust release. **Every number in the app is real.**

**Changed**

- Paper and demo balances removed — balances, positions, and PnL always reflect your real account
- The app now **fails closed**: if data can't be verified, you see an honest empty state instead of a placeholder

## v1.5.0 — October 26, 2026

The terminal grows up.

**New**

- **Bloomberg-style terminal layouts** — arrange charts, books, and panels into your own desk
- **Global status bar** — market and account state always in view

**Fixed** (v1.5.1, v1.5.2)

- Chart resizing and terminal preset initialization

## v1.4.2 — October 26, 2026

**Earn goes live.**

**New**

- **Real vault deposits** — deposit from your embedded wallet into on-chain yield vaults, receipt-verified
- **My Yield** — live position values and yield PnL on the Earn tab
- Tier 3 vaults stay locked until you pass a short risk quiz — higher risk should be a decision, not a default

## v1.4.1 — October 26, 2026

**Improved**

- Portfolio PnL computed from the real ledger — realized, unrealized, and net deposits, no fixtures
- Trade **history and CSV export** from real ledger rows
- Equity sparkline tracks your true balance over time

## v1.4.0 — October 25, 2026

The **character program** — points you can see.

**New**

- **Your character** — a pixel-art avatar that grows through 6 stages as you earn lifetime points; spendable balance stays separate
- **Gear shop** — spend points on boosters: fee charm (−10% builder fee), golden gloves (1.25x points), booster packs, and more
- Effects apply live on the ticket — discounted fees show before you confirm
- New app icon across iOS, Android, and splash screens

## v1.3.0 — October 25, 2026

A typography and density revamp, top to bottom.

**Improved**

- Tighter type scale, card radii, and tracking across every screen
- Web renders Inter with JetBrains Mono numerics — numbers align like a trading desk, not a browser default
- Density passes on the board, terminal, feed, and shell header

## v1.2.0 — October 25, 2026

**New**

- **Global ⌘K search** — markets, perps, traders, and pages, from anywhere

## v1.0.0 — October 23, 2026

The first production release of Monofin. The web app is live at [app.monofin.xyz](https://app.monofin.xyz).

**New**

- **Home board** — Events / Perps / RWA / Watchlist tabs with trending sections, resolution countdowns, and search
- **Market pages** — canonical market links built for sharing, each with a resolution rules panel, discussion, and a smart-money rail
- **Leaderboard 2.0** — board modes, category filters, **calibration badges**, and Rising Stars
- **Signal** — the live feed, web-first, with a research rail on market pages
- **Wallet** — balance, deposits, transaction history, and settings in one screen
- **Perps terminal** — live prices, order book, candles, a pro ticket, positions panel, and an all-pairs picker
- **Referral center** — live from day one

**Under the hood**

- Real authentication with per-user wallets, identical across web and mobile
- Card and bank on-ramp deposits
- Live market data across perps and event markets

**Notes**

- Earn tiers render in preview while vault integrations finalize — see [Monofin Earn](/earn/what-is-monofin-earn)
- Signal is web-first; mobile follows
- iOS and Android apps are in beta
